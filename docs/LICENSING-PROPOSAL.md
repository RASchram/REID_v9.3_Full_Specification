REID v9.3 — Licensing Proposal
Enterprise Licensing Edition | Microsoft / Google Evaluation Package

1. Purpose
This Licensing Proposal defines the rights, scope, structure, and terms under which the REID v9.3 architecture may be licensed to a major technology company. It is designed for enterprise evaluation by Microsoft, Google, or equivalent organizations.

The proposal covers licensing scope, rights granted, rights retained, exclusivity options, pricing structure, support model, and roadmap alignment.

2. Licensing Scope
The REID v9.3 licensing package includes:

• Full architecture specification  
• PACK modularization (Kernel through UC)  
• Primitive definitions (KIG, EDM, CDIA, RIL, ATS, AAG, SIL)  
• Wire schemas (Protobuf and OpenAPI)  
• Governance and conformance documentation  
• Deployment templates (AKS, Helm, Azure Policy)  
• Safety and compliance documentation  
• Epoch registry  
• Integration governor specification  

The license covers the architecture and integration surfaces, not any proprietary engine or execution layer.

3. Rights Granted
The enterprise license grants the following rights:

• Right to evaluate the REID v9.3 architecture  
• Right to integrate REID into internal systems  
• Right to deploy REID in cloud, edge, or hybrid environments  
• Right to build products that incorporate REID architecture  
• Right to extend PACKs for internal use  
• Right to create internal tooling based on REID schemas  
• Right to use REID for identity, governance, and coherence stabilization  

All granted rights apply to the architecture only.

4. Rights Retained
The following rights are retained by the creator:

• Ownership of the REID architecture  
• Ownership of all PACKs  
• Ownership of all primitives  
• Ownership of drift and coherence physics  
• Ownership of SIL internals  
• Ownership of AAG internals  
• Ownership of all future versions (v9.4, v10, etc.)  
• Right to license REID to additional partners unless exclusivity is negotiated  
• Right to publish public‑safe specifications  

No ownership transfer occurs under this license.

5. Exclusivity Options
Three exclusivity tiers are available:

Tier 1 — Non‑Exclusive License  
Microsoft or Google may use REID internally and in products.  
The creator may license REID to additional partners.

Tier 2 — Sector Exclusive License  
Microsoft or Google receives exclusive rights within a defined sector (cloud, AI safety, identity systems, etc.).  
The creator may license REID outside that sector.

Tier 3 — Full Exclusive License  
Microsoft or Google receives full exclusivity for REID v9.3 and future versions.  
This tier requires a separate negotiation and premium pricing.

6. Pricing Structure
Pricing is based on the exclusivity tier and scope of integration.

Tier 1 — Non‑Exclusive  
Annual licensing fee  
Optional support contract  
Optional roadmap collaboration

Tier 2 — Sector Exclusive  
Higher annual fee  
Optional multi‑year agreement  
Optional joint development rights

Tier 3 — Full Exclusive  
One‑time acquisition fee or multi‑year premium licensing  
Joint governance rights  
Roadmap integration  
Strategic partnership agreement

Pricing is determined during negotiation based on scope, usage, and exclusivity.

7. Support Model

Support may be included or billed separately.
