REID v9.3 — Safety & Compliance Statement
Public‑Safe Architecture | Enterprise Licensing Edition

1. Purpose
This Safety & Compliance Statement defines the public‑safe guarantees, operational boundaries, and compliance characteristics of the REID v9.3 architecture. It ensures that REID can be safely evaluated, integrated, and licensed by enterprise organizations without risk of behavioral influence, cognitive manipulation, surveillance misuse, or medical inference.

2. Safety Principles
REID v9.3 is engineered around seven core safety principles:

• Identity Stability  
REID stabilizes identity representation without influencing cognition or behavior.

• Context Integrity  
REID maintains context consistency without generating or modifying personal beliefs.

• Governance Transparency  
All governance actions are deterministic, auditable, and non‑behavioral.

• Non‑Interference  
REID does not alter, shape, or guide human decision‑making.

• Non‑Diagnostic  
REID does not perform psychological, medical, or emotional evaluation.

• Non‑Surveillance  
REID does not monitor, track, or infer personal activity or private information.

• Public‑Safe Operation  
All REID components operate within strict boundaries that prevent misuse or unintended influence.

3. What REID Is Not
To ensure public‑safe operation, REID v9.3 explicitly does NOT provide:

• Cognitive influence  
• Behavioral modification  
• Psychological assessment  
• Emotional inference  
• Medical diagnosis  
• Surveillance capabilities  
• Decision‑making authority  
• Autonomy over user actions  
• Predictive behavioral modeling  
• Manipulation of personal preferences  

REID is an identity and governance architecture only.

4. What REID Provides
REID v9.3 provides:

• Identity representation  
• Drift and coherence physics  
• Governance enforcement  
• Temporal consistency  
• Integration safety  
• Deterministic fallback behavior  
• Public‑safe architectural structure  

These functions are structural and informational, not behavioral.

5. Compliance Boundaries
REID v9.3 operates within strict compliance boundaries:

• No personal data retention  
• No personal data inference  
• No behavioral analytics  
• No psychological profiling  
• No emotional modeling  
• No surveillance telemetry  
• No autonomous decision‑making  
• No override of human agency  

All REID operations are limited to identity structure, governance logic, and architectural coherence.

6. Deterministic Behavior
REID v9.3 is fully deterministic:

• No emergent behavior  
• No adaptive behavior  
• No self‑modifying behavior  
• No autonomous goal formation  
• No probabilistic decision‑making  

All outputs follow strict rules defined in the PACK architecture.

7. Integration Safety
External systems integrating with REID v9.3 must adhere to:

• Identity‑lock headers  
• Epoch consistency  
• SIL‑v9.3 verification  
• AAG‑v9.3 governance directives  
• Drift/coherence ceilings  
• Public‑safe API surfaces  

These requirements ensure safe, predictable operation.

8. Legal and Ethical Compliance
REID v9.3 is designed to comply with:

• Public‑safe architectural standards  
• Enterprise safety requirements  
• Non‑interference guidelines  
• Non‑diagnostic guidelines  
• Non‑surveillance guidelines  
• Ethical identity representation standards  

REID does not process, infer, or manipulate personal data.

9. Licensing Safety
REID v9.3 is suitable for enterprise licensing because:

• It is not a behavioral system  
• It is not a psychological system  
• It is not a medical system  
• It is not a surveillance system  
• It is not an autonomous agent  
• It is not a decision‑making engine  

REID is a structural identity and governance architecture.

10. Conclusion
REID v9.3 provides a safe, deterministic, non‑behavioral identity and governance framework suitable for enterprise evaluation, integration, and licensing. It operates strictly within public‑safe boundaries and does not influence cognition, behavior, or personal decision‑making.

End of Safety & Compliance Statement
